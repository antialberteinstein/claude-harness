# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer by email at [antialberteinstein@gmail.com](mailto:antialberteinstein@gmail.com). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may block repeat offenders.
